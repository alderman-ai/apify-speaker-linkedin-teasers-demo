# 02 Processed/ — archived speaker folders

Each subfolder arrived here from `01 To Process/` on a successful run: the
archived `intake.md` (with the measured geometry written back) and the
assets it was built from. The finished PNG itself lives in
`03 Generated Images/<speaker-name>-final.png`, not here.

Treat as an archive: **nothing here is ever overwritten** — a re-run of a
speaker whose name is already taken is archived as `<name>-<NN>`, the
lowest free number, zero-padded, first duplicate = `01`, with the same
`NN` on the PNG in `03 Generated Images/`. Nothing is cleared or renamed
to make room. `alex-alderman/`
is the live worked example (its render is
`03 Generated Images/alex-alderman-final.png`). The demo-era speakers were
archived out of the live queue into
`04 Demo and More Help/Example Speakers/Fictional Characters/` (fictional characters and
synthetic personas) and `04 Demo and More Help/Example Speakers/Real People Stress Test/` (the
simulated-"real" stress-test speakers).

Archived files are historical records and are left as they ran: intakes
archived before the 2026-09-01 tree reorganisation still reference the old
paths (`final-output/`, `visual-templates/`, `internal/`), and every
archived intake predates the 2026-09-02 folder renaming, so it still says
`to-process/`, `processed/` and `generated-images/`. Current paths are
only ever taken from the live template and skills, never from an archive.
