# Try it yourself

The quickest way to feel the split is to make a card.

Say **"show me the demo"** to the assistant and pick **line 1**. It opens a
finished example so you can see what you are making, asks the speaker's
name, then asks you for the handful of details that vary — role, company,
topic, talk length, a short blurb — and two square pictures (a logo and a
portrait). Roughly ten minutes, start to finish.

While it runs, watch for the two halves of this folder's argument:

- **The words are yours.** Type them, or let the assistant draft them. Each
  one is counted against its budget as you go (30 characters for the name,
  39 for role and company together, 26 for the topic, 115 for the blurb). Go
  over and it tells you the count and offers a shorter version — it never
  quietly trims.
- **The picture is not.** The card, the fonts, the starfield, the frame and
  the placement were coded and measured once. Your card lands in exactly the
  same place as every card in the [gallery](gallery.md).

If anything is off — a blurb too long, a photo that isn't square — the run
stops and says exactly what to fix. That stop is the whole point: a brand
asset that is *almost* right is worse than one that waits a minute.

Your finished PNG lands in `generated-images/`, and your session runs on
its own branch, so nothing you do touches anyone else's copy.
